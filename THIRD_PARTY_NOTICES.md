## TCGdex

PokeRarity builds its local card metadata cache from the public TCGdex `cards-database` project and may use TCGdex-hosted card imagery for identification/reference.

Project: `tcgdex/cards-database`

TCGdex and its database project are separate from PokeRarity. Consult the upstream repository for its current license and attribution requirements.
